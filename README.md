This is "Taximeter For All" app <b>Privacy policy</b>

- For the needs of the taximeter, we collect your <b>Location</b> during the entire time the application is running. We do not send it anywhere, we store it locally and use only for <b>calculations</b>. The taximeter algorithms are working using your precise Location, also we show you your speed during the trip. 
- For analytics, we collect your <b>approximate and exact Location</b> and send them to <b>Google Analytics</b>.
